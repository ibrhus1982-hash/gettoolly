Free PDF Splitter

GetToolly PDF Splitter lets you extract selected pages from a PDF and create a new PDF file directly in your browser. You can select individual pages such as 1, 4, 7 or page ranges such as 2-6.

How to split a PDF

  1. Choose or drag and drop your PDF file.
  2. Preview the PDF before processing.
  3. Enter the pages or ranges you want to extract.
  4. Click Split & Download.

Private PDF processing

Your PDF is processed locally in your browser using PDF-LIB. The document is not uploaded to GetToolly.
