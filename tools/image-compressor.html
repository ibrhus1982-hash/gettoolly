About Image Compressor

GetToolly's Image Compressor is a free online tool that helps you reduce the file size of JPG, PNG and WebP images quickly and easily.

The tool processes images directly in your browser, allowing you to compress images without uploading them to a server.

How to Compress an Image

Click the upload area and select an image from your device. The tool supports JPG, PNG and WebP images.

Choose your preferred compression quality and output format, then click "Compress Image".

When compression is complete, you can see the original file size, the compressed file size and the percentage of size reduction.

Click "Download Compressed Image" to save the compressed image to your device.

Why Use GetToolly Image Compressor?

Image Compressor is designed to be fast, simple and easy to use. It does not require registration or additional software.

You can choose between WebP, JPG and PNG output formats depending on your needs.

Because the image is processed directly in your browser, your original image does not need to be uploaded to our server.

Is Image Compressor Free?

Yes. GetToolly Image Compressor is free to use and does not require an account or registration.
