What Is a Percentage?

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. The word percentage means "per hundred".

Percentages are commonly used when comparing values, calculating discounts, measuring changes and understanding statistics.

How to Calculate a Percentage

To calculate a percentage of a number, multiply the percentage by the number and divide the result by 100.

Example:

What is 20% of 150?

20 × 150 ÷ 100 = 30

Therefore, 20% of 150 is 30.

Percentage Increase

To calculate the percentage increase between an original value and a new value, subtract the original value from the new value, divide the difference by the original value and multiply by 100.

Example:

A price increases from $100 to $120.

Increase = $120 − $100 = $20

Percentage increase = 20 ÷ 100 × 100 = 20%

Percentage Decrease

To calculate a percentage decrease, subtract the new value from the original value, divide the difference by the original value and multiply by 100.

Example:

A price decreases from $200 to $150.

Decrease = $200 − $150 = $50

Percentage decrease = 50 ÷ 200 × 100 = 25%

Common Uses of Percentages

Percentages are useful in many everyday situations, including shopping discounts, taxes, grades, statistics, financial calculations and comparing changes over time.
