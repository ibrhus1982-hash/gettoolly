About Image Resizer

GetToolly's Image Resizer is a free online tool that allows you to change the width and height of JPG, PNG and WebP images quickly and easily.

The image is processed directly in your browser, so you can resize your image without uploading it to a server.

How to Resize an Image

Click the upload area and select an image from your device.

Enter the desired width and height in pixels. You can keep the aspect ratio enabled to prevent the image from becoming distorted.

Click "Resize Image" to create the resized image. When processing is complete, click "Download Resized Image" to save it.
