Temperature Conversion Formulas

Temperature conversion uses mathematical formulas to convert between Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin.

Celsius to Fahrenheit

°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32

Fahrenheit to Celsius

°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9

Celsius to Kelvin

K = °C + 273.15

Kelvin to Celsius

°C = K − 273.15

Fahrenheit to Kelvin

K = (°F − 32) × 5/9 + 273.15

Kelvin to Fahrenheit

°F = (K − 273.15) × 9/5 + 32

Temperature Conversion Examples

0°C = 32°F = 273.15 K

The freezing point of water is 0 degrees Celsius, which equals 32 degrees Fahrenheit and 273.15 Kelvin.

100°C = 212°F = 373.15 K

The boiling point of water at standard atmospheric pressure is 100 degrees Celsius, equal to 212 degrees Fahrenheit and 373.15 Kelvin.

Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin Explained

Celsius (°C) is commonly used for everyday temperature measurements in most countries around the world.

Fahrenheit (°F) is mainly used for everyday temperature measurements in the United States.

Kelvin (K) is widely used in science and physics. Unlike Celsius and Fahrenheit, Kelvin does not use a degree symbol.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply the Celsius temperature by 9/5 and then add 32. For example, 20°C is equal to 68°F.

How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

Subtract 32 from the Fahrenheit temperature and multiply the result by 5/9. For example, 68°F is equal to 20°C.

How do I convert Celsius to Kelvin?

Add 273.15 to the Celsius temperature. For example, 25°C is equal to 298.15 K.

What is 0°C in Fahrenheit?

0°C is equal to 32°F.

What is 100°C in Fahrenheit?

100°C is equal to 212°F.
