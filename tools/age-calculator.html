What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is an online tool that determines how old a person is based on their date of birth and a selected calculation date.

GetToolly's age calculator shows the result in years, months and days, making it useful when you need a more precise age calculation.

How to Calculate Your Age

Enter your date of birth, select the date on which you want to calculate your age, and click "Calculate Age."

If you want to calculate your current age, simply use today's date in the second field.

Age Calculation Example

If someone was born on January 1, 2000, their age on January 1, 2026 would be:

26 years, 0 months and 0 days

Why Use an Age Calculator?

An age calculator can be useful for checking ages for applications, forms, birthdays, eligibility requirements and personal records.
