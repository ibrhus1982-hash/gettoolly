Free Online Case Converter

Convert your text to uppercase, lowercase, title case or sentence case instantly.

What Is a Case Converter?

A case converter is an online text tool that changes the capitalization of words and sentences. It can save time when editing documents, articles, emails, social media posts and other written content.

GetToolly's free case converter supports uppercase, lowercase, title case and sentence case.

Available Text Cases

Original: hello world this is a text example

UPPERCASE: HELLO WORLD THIS IS A TEXT EXAMPLE

lowercase: hello world this is a text example

Title Case: Hello World This Is A Text Example

Sentence case: Hello world this is a text example.

How to Use the Case Converter

Paste or type your text into the text box. Then choose the capitalization format you want. The selected conversion is applied instantly.

You can copy the converted text with the Copy button and paste it into another application.

Who Can Use a Case Converter?

This tool can be useful for students, writers, bloggers, developers, marketers, social media managers and anyone who works with text.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this case converter free?

Yes. GetToolly's case converter is free to use and does not require registration.

What does uppercase mean?

Uppercase converts letters to capital letters, such as HELLO WORLD.

What does lowercase mean?

Lowercase converts letters to small letters, such as hello world.

What is Title Case?

Title Case capitalizes the main words in a phrase or title, making text suitable for headings.

Does the tool store my text?

The conversion happens directly in your browser. The tool does not need to send your text to a server.
