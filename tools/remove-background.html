Free AI Background Remover Online

Removing a background from an image is useful for product photos, profile pictures, social media graphics, online stores, presentations and many other projects. GetToolly makes the process simple: choose an image, let the browser process it, and download a transparent PNG.

You do not need to install desktop software or learn complicated photo-editing tools. The background remover is designed to work directly in a modern web browser and supports common image formats including JPG, PNG and WebP.

How to Remove a Background from an Image

  1. Choose or drag and drop your JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  2. Preview the selected image and click Remove Background.
  3. Wait while the browser analyzes the image and removes the background.
  4. Download the result as a transparent PNG.
