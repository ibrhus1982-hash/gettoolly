What Is a Word Counter?

A word counter is an online tool that calculates the number of words in a piece of text. It can be useful for students, writers, bloggers, researchers, content creators and professionals.

GetToolly's free word counter also counts characters, sentences and lines, giving you a quick overview of your text.

How to Use the Word Counter

Type or paste your text into the text box above. The counters update automatically as you write.

You do not need to create an account or install any software. The tool works directly in your browser.

Why Count Words?

Word counts are useful when writing essays, articles, blog posts, social media content, applications and other documents with length requirements.
