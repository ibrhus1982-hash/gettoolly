What Is a Time Calculator?

A time calculator is a simple tool for adding time durations expressed in hours and minutes. Instead of converting everything manually into minutes, you can enter two time values and instantly calculate their combined duration.

How to Use the Time Calculator

Enter the hours and minutes for the first time value, then enter the hours and minutes for the second value. Click the Calculate Time button to see the total duration.

  • Enter the hours for the first time.
  • Enter the minutes for the first time.
  • Enter the hours for the second time.
  • Enter the minutes for the second time.
  • Click Calculate Time.
  • View the total hours, minutes and total minutes.

Time Calculation Example

2 hours 30 minutes + 1 hour 45 minutes = 4 hours 15 minutes

The two durations equal 150 minutes and 105 minutes. Together they make 255 minutes, which is 4 hours and 15 minutes.

How Time Is Converted to Minutes

One hour contains 60 minutes. To calculate the total number of minutes in a duration, multiply the number of hours by 60 and then add the remaining minutes.

Formula

Total minutes = (Hours × 60) + Minutes

3 hours 20 minutes

(3 × 60) + 20 = 200 minutes

Common Uses for a Time Calculator

A time calculator can be useful for many everyday tasks, including tracking work hours, calculating study time, planning projects, adding exercise durations, calculating travel durations and combining multiple time intervals.

  • Calculate total working hours.
  • Add study or training sessions.
  • Calculate project or task durations.
  • Add travel and activity times.
  • Convert hours and minutes into total minutes.
