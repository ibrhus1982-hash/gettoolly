How to use the Time Calculator

Enter the hours and minutes for the first and second time values, then click Calculate Time. The calculator will add the two durations and show the total time.

Example

If the first time is 2 hours 30 minutes and the second time is 1 hour 45 minutes, the result is 4 hours 15 minutes.
