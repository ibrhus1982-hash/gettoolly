About Image to Text Converter

GetToolly's Image to Text Converter is a free online OCR tool that extracts readable text from images. You can use it to convert text inside JPG, PNG and WebP images into editable text.

The tool supports both Arabic and English OCR and is useful for screenshots, scanned documents, notes, photographs and other images containing text.

How to Extract Text from an Image

  1. Upload your JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  2. Select Arabic, English or Arabic + English.
  3. Click the Extract Text button.
  4. Wait while the OCR engine processes the image.
  5. Copy or download the extracted text.

What is OCR?

OCR stands for Optical Character Recognition. It is a technology that detects characters and words inside images and converts them into editable digital text.

Arabic OCR

The converter includes an Arabic OCR model designed to recognize Arabic characters and words. For the best results, use a clear image with high resolution, good contrast and a simple background.

Arabic text can be more difficult to recognize when the image contains very small fonts, handwriting, complex backgrounds, shadows, blur or heavily compressed text.

Tips for Better OCR Results

  • Use a high-resolution image.
  • Make sure the text is sharp and readable.
  • Avoid dark or complicated backgrounds.
  • Keep the text horizontal when possible.
  • Use Arabic mode for Arabic-only documents.
  • Use Arabic + English when the image contains both languages.
