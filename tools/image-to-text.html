About Image to Text Converter

GetToolly's Image to Text Converter is a free online OCR tool that extracts text from images quickly and easily.

You can upload JPG, PNG or WebP images and convert visible text into editable text that you can copy, modify or download.

How to Extract Text from an Image

Click the upload area and select an image from your device.

Choose the language of the text in your image. You can select English, Arabic, or English and Arabic together.

Click "Extract Text" and wait while the OCR process analyzes the image.

When processing is complete, the extracted text will appear in the text box. You can then copy or download the text.

What Is OCR?

OCR stands for Optical Character Recognition. It is a technology used to recognize printed or typed text in images and convert it into editable digital text.

OCR can be useful for extracting text from scanned documents, screenshots, photographs, notes, receipts and other images containing readable text.
